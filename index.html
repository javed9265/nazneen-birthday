My dearest Nazneen,

Written on your birthday · with all of me

Every year the world quietly celebrates you for one day, and I spend all of it feeling like it should be every day — because that is how much of my life you have made better simply by being in it.

You have this way of making small things feel enormous. A message at 2 am. The sound of your laugh when something is only slightly funny. The way you remember the tiny details about people and keep them safe like they matter — because to you, they do.

I hope this year is unreasonably kind to you. I hope you get the doors that open easily, the plans that don't fall apart, and the kind of happiness that makes you stop and think, "wow, I'm really happy right now."

And when it isn't kind — because some days won't be — I hope you remember that you are the most stubbornly loved person I know. You don't have to earn it. You never did.

Thank you for being exactly, impossibly you.

Always yours
